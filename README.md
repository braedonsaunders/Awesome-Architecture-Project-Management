# Awesome-Architecture-Project-Management

# Top Architecture Project Management Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on A/E Firm Project Management, Phase-Based Budgeting, Time & Billing, Resource Planning & Practice Management for Architecture and Engineering*  
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Architecture Project Management**. These systems help architecture and engineering (A/E) firms manage projects by design phase, track time and fees, handle billing (including AIA-style formats), plan resources, and run the business side of professional practice.

**Examples** include Monograph, Deltek Ajera, BQE CORE, ArchiOffice, Factor AE, WorkflowMax, BigTime, Unanet, Projectworks, and Scoro (the category leaders and adjacent professional-services platforms).

**Open-source emphasis**: Purpose-built A/E practice-management platforms with phase-based budgeting, AIA-aligned billing, and firm-specific workflows are almost exclusively commercial. Open-source activity is limited to emerging conceptual projects and general open project-management / ERP tools that firms can adapt. This section lists every significant relevant effort found.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Monograph](https://monograph.com/)**  
  Purpose-built project management and financial visibility platform designed specifically for architecture and engineering firms—phase-based planning, time tracking, budgeting, and invoicing with strong visual tools (e.g., MoneyGantt).

- **[Deltek Ajera](https://www.deltek.com/)**  
  Integrated project management and accounting suite tailored for architecture and engineering firms, covering project tracking, time, billing, and light payroll/operations.

- **[BQE CORE](https://www.bqe.com/)**  
  End-to-end firm management platform created with architects and engineers in mind—phase-based budgeting, resource management, CRM, time & billing, and optional accounting/payroll modules.

- **[Factor AE, ArchiOffice, Projectworks](https://www.factorae.com/)**  
  Specialized or mid-market tools focused on A/E project delivery, time tracking, and practice operations.

- **[BigTime, Unanet, WorkflowMax, Scoro](https://www.bigtime.net/)**  
  Professional-services platforms widely used by architecture, engineering, and related firms for project management, time, billing, and resource planning.

- **[Other commercial A/E and professional-services platforms](https://monograph.com/)**  
  Additional solutions supporting fee-by-phase tracking, AIA-style invoicing, and firm-wide project visibility.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Architecture Firm Management (conceptual / emerging)](https://github.com/worlds-biggest-software-project/325-architecture-firm-management)**  
  Open-source initiative aiming to deliver an AI-native practice-management platform for architecture and engineering firms—phase-based project tracking, time and billing, design deliverables, and client portal concepts. Still early-stage.

- **[Engineering Firm Project Management (related initiative)](https://github.com/worlds-biggest-software-project/326-engineering-firm-project-management)**  
  Companion open effort targeting engineering consultancies with phase-based budgeting, flexible billing models, resource scheduling, and deliverable coordination.

- **[General open-source project management platforms](https://github.com/search?q=open+source+project+management)**  
  Mature tools such as OpenProject, Plane, Taiga, and similar systems that can be configured for project tracking, time logging, and team collaboration (require customization for A/E phase and fee structures).

- **[Open ERP & professional-services modules](https://github.com/search?q=ERPNext+OR+Odoo+project+billing)**  
  ERPNext, Odoo, and related open ERP platforms with project, timesheet, and invoicing modules that some firms adapt for architecture practice management.

- **[Time tracking & timesheet open tools](https://github.com/search?q=time+tracking+OR+timesheet+open+source)**  
  Self-hosted time-tracking applications that can feed custom billing and utilization reporting.

- **[AIA / construction billing helpers](https://github.com/search?q=AIA+G702+OR+schedule+of+values)**  
  Community scripts and templates related to AIA-style progress billing formats that can be integrated into custom workflows.

- **[Resource scheduling open components](https://github.com/search?q=resource+scheduling+OR+capacity+planning+open+source)**  
  Libraries and simple applications for staffing and capacity planning across projects.

- **[AEC collaboration & data open tools](https://github.com/search?q=Speckle+OR+AEC+open+source)**  
  Open platforms (e.g., Speckle) focused on design data and collaboration that complement, rather than replace, practice-management systems.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Emerging A/E-specific efforts**: Early open initiatives explicitly targeting architecture and engineering firm management.
- **Configurable open PM + ERP**: OpenProject or ERPNext configured with custom phases, fee types, and billing rules.
- **Composable stacks**: Time tracking + project boards + invoicing tools + accounting export for smaller practices.
- **Design-data layer**: Speckle and similar open AEC tools for model and deliverable coordination alongside financial systems.
- Full commercial A/E practice platforms remain far more mature for phase-based fees, AIA billing, and firm-wide financial visibility.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  
There is currently no mature, production-ready open-source equivalent to Monograph, Deltek Ajera, or BQE CORE.  
Emerging conceptual projects aim to fill this gap. In the meantime, firms can adapt **OpenProject**, **ERPNext/Odoo**, and open time-tracking tools with custom fields for design phases, fee types, and utilization.  
Commercial platforms deliver polished phase-based budgeting, AIA-aligned invoicing, resource planning, and industry-specific workflows that most architecture and engineering practices rely on.  
Small firms or those with strong internal technical capacity may assemble workable open stacks; mid-size and larger practices typically choose purpose-built commercial A/E project management software.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Architecture project management systems often handle sensitive financial, client, and project data. Ensure appropriate security, access controls, and (where relevant) compliance with professional and contractual requirements.
- Open-source tools offer flexibility and zero licensing cost but require configuration, hosting, and ongoing maintenance to approximate the phase-based and billing workflows that commercial A/E platforms provide out of the box. Evaluate total cost of ownership and fit carefully.
